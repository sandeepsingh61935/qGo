Claim → lease → ack

Compress this. Speak it. Do not invent “exactly-once.”

Guarantee (say this)

At-least-once delivery + idempotent handlers. Not exactly-once end-to-end.

Crash mid-job

  1. Claim already happened → job in processing
  2. No ack → not deleted
  3. Lease expires → reaper requeues
  4. Another worker may run it again
